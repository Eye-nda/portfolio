About

From script to screen, from wall to audience

I trained as a screenwriter and director at AFDA, and have spent the years since moving between writers' rooms, film sets, gallery walls and campaign briefs. As a writer I worked on Mzansi Magic's Champions across two seasons; as a script supervisor I helped bring Recipes for Love and Murder to screen for M-Net and Acorn TV.

Alongside that, I make photographs — portraiture, documentary and conceptual work that sits with Black interiority, masculinity, memory, belonging and cultural identity. I'm less interested in documenting a subject than in building a visual language that is distinctly South African.

Between productions I've worked front of house at Southern Guild, curated at FEDE Arthouse, and advised artists and brands through Siyababa INDUKU. I understand a project from inside the edit suite and inside the gallery — that's what I bring into every collaboration.
